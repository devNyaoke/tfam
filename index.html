Who We Are

TFAM Global is a global community of faith-rooted justice activists advancing dignity and equity for all people. We believe faith and justice are inextricable, grounded in the call to love our neighbors and care for the least of humanity.

We advocate for LGBTQIA+ justice, gender equity, and health equity by challenging systems of harm and exclusion, reflecting God's compassionate and inclusive love, especially toward those made vulnerable by economic injustice, oppression, and social exclusion.

Working across the UK and Europe, Latin America and the Caribbean, Central America, East and West Africa, with collaborative partners in Asia and South Africa, we transform faith into action for justice across cultures, languages, and geographies.

TFAM Holy Convocation 25

TFAM Global Marked the 25th Holy Convocation in Atlanta, gathering faith leaders and communities during a pivotal political moment described by organizers as “such a time as this.” The historic week centered healing, celebration, liberation, and spiritual renewal through worship, workshops, and legacy-focused programming, reaffirming a shared conviction in faith, justice, and collective hope.
