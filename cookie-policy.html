1. What Are Cookies?

+

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

Important: Cookies do not contain personal information unless you have specifically provided it. They cannot access other files on your computer or device, and they cannot carry viruses or malware.

4. Managing Cookies

+

Browser Settings

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

Google Chrome

  1. Click the three dots menu (⋮)
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy and security
  3. Click Cookies and other site data
  4. Choose your cookie preferences

Mozilla Firefox

  1. Click the menu button (≡)
  2. Select Settings → Privacy & Security
  3. Choose cookie preferences under Cookies and Site Data

Safari

  1. Go to Safari → Preferences
  2. Click the Privacy tab
  3. Adjust settings under Website tracking

Impact of Disabling Cookies: Some features may not work properly if you disable cookies. You may need to re-enter information and your preferences may not be saved.
