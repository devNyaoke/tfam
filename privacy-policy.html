When you visit our Site, we may automatically collect certain information about your device and usage patterns:

5. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your personal information:

5.1 Technical Safeguards

  • Encryption: SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
  • Secure Servers: Data stored on secure servers with restricted access
  • Firewalls: Network security firewalls and intrusion detection systems

5.2 Organizational Safeguards

  • Access controls limiting who can view your information

Important: While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but continuously work to maintain the highest standards of data protection.
