1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

Cookies typically contain:

  • The name of the website that created the cookie
  • A unique identifier for your device
  • An expiration date (some cookies last only for the duration of your session, while others persist)
  • Information about your preferences or activities on the site

Important: Cookies do not contain personal information unless you have specifically provided it. They cannot access other files on your computer or device, and they cannot carry viruses or malware.

5. How to Manage Cookies

You have several options for controlling and managing cookies:

5.1 Our Cookie Consent Tool

When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner allowing you to:

  • Accept All: Allow all cookies including analytics and functional cookies
  • Essential Only: Allow only essential cookies required for the site to function
  • Customize: View this policy and make informed choices

Note: You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting our site, which will trigger the consent banner again.

5.2 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. Here's how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

Google Chrome

  1. Click the three dots menu (⋮) in the top right
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy and security
  3. Click Cookies and other site data
  4. Choose your preferred cookie settings

Mozilla Firefox

  1. Click the menu button (≡) and select Settings
  2. Select Privacy & Security
  3. Under Cookies and Site Data, choose your preferences

Safari (Mac)

  1. Go to Safari → Preferences
  2. Click the Privacy tab
  3. Adjust cookie settings under Website tracking

Microsoft Edge

  1. Click the three dots menu (⋯) and select Settings
  2. Go to Privacy, search, and services
  3. Under Cookies and site permissions, manage cookies

5.3 Mobile Device Settings

iOS (Safari)

  1. Go to Settings → Safari
  2. Scroll to Privacy & Security
  3. Toggle Block All Cookies or adjust tracking settings

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open Chrome app
  2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) → Settings
  3. Tap Site settings → Cookies
  4. Choose your cookie preferences

6. Impact of Disabling Cookies

Blocking or deleting cookies may affect your experience on our website:

Potential Issues with Disabled Cookies:

  • You may need to re-enter information more frequently
  • Some website features may not function properly
  • Personalization features will be disabled
  • You may see the cookie consent banner repeatedly
  • Your preferences may not be saved between visits
  • Forms may not work correctly

Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are necessary for the website to function. However, you can choose to stop using our website if you do not want these cookies.

7. Local Storage and Similar Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use HTML5 local storage and similar technologies for:

  • Storing newsletter popup preferences

Local storage data can be cleared through your browser settings, typically in the same location where you manage cookies.

8. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you do not want your online activities tracked.

Currently, there is no industry standard for how websites should respond to DNT signals. We do not currently respond to DNT browser signals, but we respect your privacy choices made through our cookie consent tool.
