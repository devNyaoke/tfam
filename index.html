Who We Are

TFAM Global is a global community of faith-rooted justice activists advancing dignity and equity for all people. We believe faith and justice are inextricable, grounded in the call to love our neighbors and care for the least of humanity.

We advocate for LGBTQIA+ justice, gender equity, and health equity by challenging systems of harm and exclusion, reflecting God's compassionate and inclusive love, especially toward those made vulnerable by economic injustice, oppression, and social exclusion.

Working across the UK and Europe, Latin America and the Caribbean, Central America, East and West Africa, with collaborative partners in Asia and South Africa, we transform faith into action for justice across cultures, languages, and geographies.

Join us for monthly Faith Connect Africa sermons every third week of the month, featuring diverse voices, inspiring messages, and faith-rooted conversations that strengthen solidarity, nurture belonging, and celebrate affirming theology across the continent. Wawa Aba Conference 2026

Unapologetically Sacred: Reclaiming Bodies, Power, and Prophecy. From November 11–13, 2026, we will gather in Nairobi, Kenya to explore what it means to reclaim our bodies, our power, and our prophetic voices. Through womanist theology, courageous conversations, worship, learning, and community, we will create space to name what has been silenced, honour what has been sacred, and imagine new possibilities for liberation. Come ready to reflect, reconnect, learn, and boldly embrace the sacredness of who we are and the work we are called to do. Unapologetically Sacred. Reclaiming Bodies. Power. Prophecy.
